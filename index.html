Asian Aviation (Asian Group) Privacy & Data Protection Guidelines

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry, request a flight quote, or book travel packages through Asian Aviation, we collect personal information such as your name, contact phone number, email address, passport information (when required for ticketing or visas), and travel dates.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used strictly to process flight reservations, secure hotel bookings, prepare visa documentation, coordinate pilgrimage itineraries, and communicate directly regarding your trip.

3. Data Security & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information is only shared with relevant airlines, hotels, embassies/consulates, and ground handling partners strictly necessary to fulfill your booked services.
